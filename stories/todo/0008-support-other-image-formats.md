# Story: Support image formats other than .jpg

Status: Not yet example-mapped.

Sliced out of [0006-show-photo-instruction](0006-show-photo-instruction.md) during its Example Mapping session.

## Context

In story 0006, only lowercase `.jpg` files can be shown with a SHOW instruction (and `/list` and `/images` accept only `.jpg`). Other formats or extensions (e.g. `.JPG`, `.jpeg`, `.png`) might be supported in future.
